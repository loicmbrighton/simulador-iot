- **Z-Anatomy, the libre 3D atlas of anatomy**: CC-BY-SA 4.0. Gauthier Kervyn (design, 3D, anatomia),
  Marcin Zielinski (add-on), Lluís Vinent. https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/The-blend
